# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want dotcontent to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and languages.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and
  learn from them.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[dotmirror.com](https://dotmirror.com). Every report is reviewed and kept
confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that
break this code. They may also ban a contributor for a time or permanently.
